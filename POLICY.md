# Privacy Policy for Smart Ledger

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026

Smart Ledger ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

## Information Collection and Use
Smart Ledger is a self-hosted family inventory management application. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data on external centralized servers. 

- **Camera Data:** The application utilizes the device's camera strictly and exclusively for local barcode scanning (such as product OEM numbers and item barcodes). Camera images and video feeds are processed in real-time on your device and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted to any third party.
- **Local Storage:** All inventory records, item details, and preferences are stored locally on your device or synchronized solely through your personal configured database/backend (such as Firebase), completely under your control.

## Third-Party Services
The app may utilize standard device APIs or external open product databases (e.g., Open Food Facts) solely for fetching public item metadata based on scanned barcodes. No personal user information is shared with these services.

## Children's Privacy
Our application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
